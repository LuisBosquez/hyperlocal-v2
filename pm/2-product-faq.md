# Frequently asked questions about hyperlocal
These are FAQs that provide supplementary information to guide the feature proposal of this application. 

## Why does your product exist?
It’s 2026, and we still rely on text-based logistics and planning when we go out. Organizers jump between Maps, Calendar and messaging apps, spending a large amount of thankless effort and incurring in a lot of risks and potential for errors.

## Why are the current tools not good enough?
- Google Maps: has too much noise. It's a combination of too many things: A places database of all kinds of places, a route and directions planner with multiple different means of transportation, and most commonly, and ad platform. 
- Google Calendar/Outlook/Any other calendar: Needs organizers to input every single detail of their event before sending the invitation. It's inflexible and leads to non-organic communication.
- Partiful: More delightful than calendar events, but lacks a tight integration with places. 

## Where are the best events and things to do coming from? 
The best things to do come from ephemeral sources and underground comms: Local newsletters (e.g. The Seattle Theater Group community newsletter), Instagram stories of new places, Instagram/Facebook posts, Facebook Groups, private group chats, word of mouth, etc. 

## What can be solved in the future?
The ecosystem lacks an algorithm for intelligent outing recommendations based on our preferences and the guest’s, external variables related to the space-time of the events, and changes in plans. The latter is as big of a necessity as the need to meet itself. The amount of change and variables that humans encounter on a daily basis amplifies the need for flexibility with plans and commitments. This makes it necessary for flexibility to be an integral part of whatever application aims to solve planning and organizing.
